Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 June 2026

1. Who we are

IgniteResults provides internal analytics services to track the performance and costs of Facebook marketing campaigns. Our tools are used only to monitor and improve advertising KPIs and expenses.

2. Information we collect

We use the Facebook API to access advertising and marketing data only. Specifically, we may collect:

  • Ad campaign, ad set, and ad names and IDs
  • Impressions, clicks, conversions, and similar performance metrics
  • Spend, cost per result, and related financial metrics
  • Targeting, placements, and scheduling information related to your ads

We do not use the Facebook API to access or store:

  • Personal profile information of individual Facebook users
  • Private messages or posts
  • Friends lists or contact information
  • Sensitive categories of personal data (such as health, religion, or political opinions)

Any personal data in the ad reporting we receive is limited and aggregated (for example, anonymized or aggregated performance metrics).

3. How we use the information

We use the advertising data we obtain from the Facebook API solely to:

  • Track and analyze the performance of ad campaigns
  • Calculate and monitor advertising costs and KPIs
  • Generate internal reports and dashboards for our clients or internal stakeholders
  • Improve ad strategy and budget allocation

We do not sell, rent, or license this data to third parties.

4. Legal basis and permissions

We access Facebook marketing data using the appropriate permissions granted through Facebook’s developer platform and Facebook’s Marketing / Ads APIs. By connecting your Facebook ad account or Business Manager to our tools, you authorize IgniteResults to retrieve this information on your behalf for the purposes described above.

5. Data sharing

We may share advertising performance data in the following limited ways:

  • With our clients or internal stakeholders, for reporting and analytics
  • With service providers (such as hosting, analytics, or storage providers) who help us operate our tools and who are bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations
  • When required by law, regulation, or a valid legal request from authorities

We do not share Facebook user‑level personal data with any unrelated third parties.

6. Data storage and security

We store ad performance data in secure systems with reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect it from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. These measures may include:

  • Access controls and authentication
  • Encryption in transit and at rest (where applicable)
  • Regular backups and monitoring

We retain data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy or as required by law, and then delete or anonymize it.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on your relationship with us and your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the data we store about your ad accounts
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of certain data where applicable
  • Withdraw access by disconnecting your Facebook account or revoking the app’s permissions in your Facebook settings

If you wish to exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below. You can also manage or revoke our access to your Facebook ad data directly in your Facebook Business / App settings.

8. Third‑party services

Our tools may be hosted on or integrated with third‑party services (for example, cloud infrastructure, databases, or analytics platforms). These services process data on our behalf and are contractually obligated to protect it and use it only according to our instructions.

Your use of Facebook is also governed by Facebook’s own terms and policies, including Facebook’s Data Policy and any applicable terms for advertisers.

9. International transfers

If our infrastructure or service providers are located outside your country, your ad data may be transferred and processed in other countries that may have different data protection laws. In such cases, we take steps to ensure an appropriate level of protection in line with applicable legal requirements.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top. Continued use of our tools after changes are posted means you accept the revised policy.
